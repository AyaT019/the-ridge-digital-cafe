# The Ridge Digital Elixir

I want to make a dejital coffe menu for "the ridge"  here is the original menu and photos so u understandt the theme. Café atmosphere section

Between menu categories, add occasional full-width photography sections.

For example:

A beautiful photo of the café interior with the text:

"Take your time."

Then continue into the next menu category.

This should make the menu feel like a luxury brand experience rather than a simple digital price list.

Footer

Use a dark espresso-brown footer with:

Café logo
Opening hours
Location
Phone
Instagram
Google Maps

Keep it minimal and elegant.

Important design rule

Photography is a PRIMARY part of the website.

The final result should visually communicate:

Luxury
Quality
Taste
Warmth
Elegance
Exclusivity

The customer should want to order something partly because the photography makes it look irresistible.

Do NOT make the website look like an ordinary restaurant ordering system.

Make it feel like a luxury café's digital brand magazine.

Use realistic sample products, Tunisian dinar prices, beautiful food photography, sophisticated typography, elegant spacing, and polished animations.

Make the entire experience responsive, premium, and production-ready.
